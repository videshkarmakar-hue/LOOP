# Loop

### Download Everything. From Everywhere.

**Loop** is an all-in-one, multi-platform content downloader designed to make downloading media from popular social and entertainment platforms simple and convenient.

Download content from platforms like **Facebook, Instagram, YouTube**, and many more — all through one unified application.

Built to bring multiple platforms together in a clean, simple, and user-friendly downloading experience.

---

## About Loop

Loop aims to simplify the way you download and manage online media.

Instead of switching between different downloader websites or applications, Loop brings multi-platform downloading capabilities together in one convenient place.

Whether it's a video, audio, or other supported media, Loop focuses on providing an accessible and straightforward experience.

**Simple. Universal. Convenient.**

## Highlights

- **Multi-Platform Support** — Download supported content from Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, and many more platforms.
- **All-in-One Downloader** — Access multiple platform download capabilities in one application.
- **Video Downloading** — Save supported online videos to your device.
- **Audio Downloading** — Download audio where supported.
- **Simple Experience** — An intuitive interface designed for easy navigation.
- **Fast & Convenient** — A streamlined downloading process with minimal steps.
- **Modern Design** — A clean interface focused on usability and accessibility.
- **Growing Platform Support** — More supported platforms and improvements may arrive in future releases.
